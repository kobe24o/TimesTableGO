# TimesTable GO Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last updated / 更新日期: October 19, 2026 / 2026 年 10 月 19 日

## English

TimesTable GO is an offline multiplication practice app developed by Mingming Chen.

### Information handled on your device
The iOS app has no account registration, advertising, analytics SDK, or developer-operated server. The developer does not collect personal information, audio, practice answers, or learning statistics through the app. Settings and practice records are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app may delete this local information.

### Microphone and speech recognition
The app requests microphone access when you start voice practice. Audio is processed on your device using the bundled speech recognition model to recognize your answer and provide feedback. Audio is held temporarily in memory; the app does not save recordings or upload audio to the developer or a speech service. You can revoke microphone access in iOS Settings. Voice answering requires this permission.

### Purchases
The iOS app is a one-time paid download through the App Store. Apple handles payment under its own privacy policy. The developer does not receive your payment-card information. There are no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

### Children and tracking
The app does not collect personal information from children, track users across apps or websites, or provide social features, chat, advertising, or publicly shared user content.

### Your choices and contact
You can stop practice at any time, change microphone permissions in iOS Settings, and remove locally stored app data by deleting the app. For support or privacy questions, contact mingmingchen1989@gmail.com or visit https://github.com/kobe24o/TimesTableGO/issues. Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated date.

## 简体中文

TimesTable GO 是由 Mingming Chen 开发的离线乘法练习应用。

### 设备本地数据
本 iOS 应用无需注册账号，不使用广告、分析 SDK 或开发者运营的服务器。开发者不会通过应用收集个人信息、录音、答题内容或学习统计。设置和练习记录保存在设备本地，卸载应用可能删除这些本地数据。

### 麦克风和语音识别
开始语音练习时，应用会请求麦克风权限。音频通过内置语音模型在设备本机处理，用于识别答案和提供反馈。音频只在内存中短暂使用，不保存为录音文件，也不上传至开发者或语音服务。你可以在 iOS 设置中关闭麦克风权限，关闭后无法使用语音作答。

### 购买
本 iOS 应用通过 App Store 一次付费下载。付款由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理，开发者不会收到支付卡信息。应用不含订阅或应用内购买。

### 儿童与跟踪
应用不收集儿童个人信息，不跨应用或网站跟踪用户，不提供社交、聊天、广告或公开分享用户内容的功能。

### 你的选择与联系我们
你可以随时停止练习、在 iOS 设置中管理麦克风权限，或通过删除应用移除本地数据。有关支持或隐私的问题，请联系 mingmingchen1989@gmail.com，或访问 https://github.com/kobe24o/TimesTableGO/issues。本政策变更时会更新页面日期。
